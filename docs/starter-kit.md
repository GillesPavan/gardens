# Gardens — Starter Kit

> Work in progress. This document will contain ready-to-use models for participants who are starting from zero or with little knowledge of indicators.

## Goal

Eliminate the friction of modeling by providing simple, adaptable starter models.

## Target Audiences

1. **Starting from zero**: people with a garden but no experience with indicators or modeling.
2. **Already tracking things**: people who already keep a gardening journal, track yields, or observe their garden regularly.
3. **Want to improve**: people with an established garden looking for new levers and collective learning.

## Starter Model Principles

- Simple: start with a few indicators, not dozens.
- Adaptable: every garden is different.
- Progressive: add complexity only when it brings value.
- Collective: the model improves with the group.
