# Gardens — Landing Page Structure

## Positioning

Gardens is the first concrete application of Adaequa's quality modeling approach. It is designed to be accessible to people with little or no prior knowledge of indicators or modeling.

## Core Promise

> "We give you a method and starter indicators — not a blank page."

## Landing Page Structure

### 1. Hero

- **Title**: *Gardens — Cultivating quality, together*
- **Subtitle**: A guided project to define, measure, and evolve the indicators that really matter in an organic garden — with starter models and a group that progresses step by step.
- **Primary CTA**: *Find my starting point*
- **Secondary CTA**: *See how it works*

### 2. The Challenge

> "Every garden is unique. But how do you know if it's 'doing well' — and how can participants collectively improve without reducing everything to yield?"

### 3. "What is your situation?" — Routing Section

Three clickable cards leading to slightly different journeys:

| Situation | Promise | CTA |
|---|---|---|
| **Starting from zero** | No experience with indicators? We give you a simple starter model to adapt. | *Start with the model* |
| **I already track things** | Already have data or practices? Integrate them into the common model and enrich it. | *Integrate my practice* |
| **I want to improve** | Your garden is running, but you want to go further? Compare and identify levers. | *Identify my levers* |

### 4. The Common Principle

> "Whatever your starting point, you are part of the same project: collectively defining what makes a quality organic garden, and evolving those indicators together."

### 5. How It Works

1. Choose your situation.
2. Adapt the model to your garden.
3. Collect a few observations.
4. Share, compare, and refine.

### 6. What the Project Provides

- A starter set of qualitative and quantitative indicators.
- A simple observation format (text, photo, rating).
- A realistic collection rhythm (monthly or seasonal).
- A space to exchange with other participants.

### 7. What We Learn Together

- **For my garden**: seeing beyond yield — biodiversity, soil health, resilience, enjoyment.
- **For the group**: building a shared language around quality.
- **For the method**: showing that quality modeling is accessible even without expertise.

### 8. Adaequa Link

> "Gardens is the first application of Adaequa's quality modeling approach. The goal is to make this approach accessible to everyone, including those starting from zero."

### 9. Final CTA

- **Primary**: *Find my starting point*
- **Secondary**: *Stay updated*

## Notes

- Prefer **Option 2** for displaying the routing: hero first, then routing section.
- Keep one idea per section.
- Explain domain words like "coopetition" and "qualitative indicators" in one line.
- Use visuals: a simple schema of the three pillars helps more than a long text.
