# Gardens

Coopetitive quality indicators for organic gardens.

> A concrete case study of qualitative evolution modeling, applied to a single organic garden of fixed size, with co-exploration and co-evolution across all participants' gardens.

## Purpose

Gardens is a practical implementation of Adaequa's quality modeling approach. It aims to make the concepts of qualitative evolution, co-exploration, and co-evolution tangible through a project that is accessible, concrete, and easy to join.

## Why Gardens?

- **Accessible entry point**: You don't need to be an expert in indicators or ecology to start. We provide starter models and a step-by-step framework.
- **Coopetition**: Gardeners share observations and learn from each other without sterile competition.
- **Co-evolution**: The group collectively refines indicators and practices season after season.
